About

Technical range.
Human context.

I’m a UC Santa Cruz computer science graduate with an applied mathematics minor, and I’m currently moving toward a career in medical imaging while continuing to build software independently.

My background spans startups, education, hotel guest services, and hospital volunteering. That mix taught me something pure engineering environments often miss: software only matters when it respects the people using it.

I’m strongest when a problem is messy, the information is scattered, and the product needs to become obvious. I enjoy turning that chaos into search tools, workflows, and interfaces that feel direct instead of over-engineered.

01Start with the user’s friction.

Features are worthless when they do not remove a real obstacle.

02Use the smallest reliable stack.

Complexity has to earn its place.

03Ship, observe, improve.

A working product teaches more than another month of polishing in private.
